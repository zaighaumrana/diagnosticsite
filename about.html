ABOUT REAL DIAGNOSTIC CENTER

One identity for laboratory and pharmacy care.

Real Diagnostic Center is the shared home of Real Lab Lahore and Real Pharmacy Lahore, serving patients from Munir Chowk, Civil Hospital Road, Gujranwala.

OUR PURPOSE

Make essential health services easier to understand.

Laboratory tests and medicines often form connected parts of a patient’s care. Real Diagnostic Center brings both services under one clear identity while keeping the work of the laboratory and pharmacy distinct.

This website is designed primarily for awareness. It explains common service areas, helps people prepare useful questions, and provides confirmed contact details before an in-person visit.
