PHARMACY SUPPORT

Simple help for everyday health needs.

Medicine delivery and online ordering are not offered through this website. Please contact the pharmacy for current availability.

01

Prescription medicines

Bring a valid prescription where required. The pharmacy team can help you understand the supplied medicine and its label.

02

Over-the-counter products

Ask about common non-prescription products and when professional medical advice may be more appropriate.

03

First-aid essentials

In-person access to common first-aid and basic care products, subject to availability.

04

Wellness essentials

Ask about vitamins, personal care, and general wellness products suitable for your needs.

USE MEDICINES SAFELY

A medicine is only helpful when it is used correctly.

Follow the instructions from your doctor and pharmacist. Check the label, dose, timing, storage, and expiry date. Do not share prescription medicines with another person.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Call with the exact medicine details.

Share the medicine name, strength, and dosage form shown on the prescription or package. This helps the team check the correct item.

01

Keep the prescription ready

A clear photo may help while confirming the medicine name, but bring the original when required.

02

Confirm the strength

The same medicine may be available in different strengths or forms. Do not substitute on your own.

03

Ask before combining medicines

Tell the pharmacist about other medicines you use and ask a qualified professional if unsure.
